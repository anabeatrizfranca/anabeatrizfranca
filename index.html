PAPER · ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE · WORK

Quantity vs. Quality: How Artificial Intelligence and Immediate-Reward Culture are Reshaping the Workforce

This paper examines how artificial intelligence and immediate-reward culture are reshaping the modern workforce.

Drawing on literature from psychology, neuroscience, organizational behavior and labor economics, the study explores how speed, instant gratification and short-term results influence motivation, learning and career development.

The research also examines purpose-driven careers, skills-based hiring, signaling theory and the distinction between deep and surface learning.
